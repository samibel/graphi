Use ValidArgsFunction and RegisterFlagCompletionFunc() for custom completions portable across Bash, Zsh, Fish, and PowerShell. The documentation explicitly recommends these APIs and says legacy Bash-script custom completions, including BashCompCustom, are ignored for Zsh (site/content/completions/_index.md:477-478). Cobra can generate completion files for those four shells (site/content/user_guide.md:742-743).
