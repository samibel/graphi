A child command inherits persistent flags by merging flag sets before use. In `command.go:1847-1853`, `mergePersistentFlags()`:

1. Calls `c.updateParentsPflags()` to collect missing persistent flags from all parents.
2. Adds the child’s own `c.PersistentFlags()` to `c.Flags()`.
3. Adds the collected `c.parentsPflags` to `c.Flags()`.

Individual lookup follows the same chain: `persistentFlag` first checks the current command’s persistent flags, then updates and searches `parentsPflags` (`command.go:1805-1816`). Non-persistent local flags do not propagate, as documented and filtered by `LocalNonPersistentFlags()` (`command.go:1654-1666`).
