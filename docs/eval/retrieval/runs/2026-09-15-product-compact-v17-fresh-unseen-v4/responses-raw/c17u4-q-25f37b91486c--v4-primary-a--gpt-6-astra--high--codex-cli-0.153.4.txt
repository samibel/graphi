In `command.go`, `(*Command).IsAvailableCommand` (lines 1559–1576) makes a command unavailable if:

- `len(c.Deprecated) != 0` or `c.Hidden`.
- It has a parent and is that parent’s help command: `c.Parent().helpCommand == c`.
- It is neither runnable nor has any available subcommands.

After passing the first two exclusions, it is available when **`c.Runnable() || c.HasAvailableSubCommands()`**:

- `Runnable()` means `c.Run != nil || c.RunE != nil` (lines 1549–1553).
- `HasAvailableSubCommands()` means at least one subcommand’s `IsAvailableCommand()` returns true (lines 1614–1628).
