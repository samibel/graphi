`UseLine` appends `" [flags]"` when:

- `DisableFlagsInUseLine` is false,
- `c.HasAvailableFlags()` is true, meaning there are non-hidden, non-deprecated local or inherited persistent flags, and
- the usage line does not already contain `"[flags]"`.

Evidence: `command.go:1436-1450` (`UseLine`) and `command.go:1770-1775` (`HasAvailableFlags`).
