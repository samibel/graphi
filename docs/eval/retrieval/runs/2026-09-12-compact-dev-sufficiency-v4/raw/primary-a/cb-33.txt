`(*Command).Flags() *flag.FlagSet` is documented as returning the complete set applying to a command, including local and persistent flags declared there or by parents (`command.go:1641–1643`). Its body lazily creates `c.flags` with `flag.NewFlagSet(c.Name(), flag.ContinueOnError)`, initializes an error buffer if needed, sends flag output to that buffer, and returns `c.flags` (`command.go:1644–1653`). The shown body does not itself merge inherited flags; `InheritedFlags()` calls `mergePersistentFlags()` and is documented as returning parent-inherited flags (`command.go:1695–1703`). `LocalNonPersistentFlags()` represents command-specific flags that do not persist to children (`command.go:1655–1660`).
