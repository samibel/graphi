Unknown-command suggestions are built from available child commands. `SuggestionsFor` adds a child when its name is within `SuggestionsMinimumDistance` using case-insensitive Levenshtein distance, when its lowercase name has the lowercase input as a prefix, or when the input case-insensitively equals one of the child's explicit `SuggestFor` entries (`command.go:831-851`). `findSuggestions` returns nothing when disabled, defaults a nonpositive distance to 2, and formats matches under `Did you mean this?` (`command.go:750-766`).
