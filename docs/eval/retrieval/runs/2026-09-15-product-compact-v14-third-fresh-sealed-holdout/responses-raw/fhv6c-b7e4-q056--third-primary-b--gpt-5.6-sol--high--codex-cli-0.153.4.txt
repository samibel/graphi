`Command.SetOutput(output io.Writer)` sets both `c.outWriter` and `c.errWriter` to the same writer; it is deprecated in favor of `SetOut`/`SetErr` ([command.go:280–286]). Output resolution uses the command’s writer, then inherits from its parent, then falls back to the supplied default ([command.go:394–402]). A `nil` output ultimately defaults usage and error messages to `os.Stderr` ([command.go:280–282]).
