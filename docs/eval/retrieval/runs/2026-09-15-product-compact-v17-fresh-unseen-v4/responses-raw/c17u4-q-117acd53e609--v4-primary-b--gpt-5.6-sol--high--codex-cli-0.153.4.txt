`getOut` checks writers in this order:

1. The command’s own `c.outWriter`, if non-nil.
2. The parent command’s writer recursively via `c.parent.getOut(def)`, if a parent exists.
3. The supplied default writer `def`.

Evidence: `command.go:393–402`, symbol `(*Command).getOut`. `HasParent` means `c.parent != nil` (`command.go:1630–1634`).
