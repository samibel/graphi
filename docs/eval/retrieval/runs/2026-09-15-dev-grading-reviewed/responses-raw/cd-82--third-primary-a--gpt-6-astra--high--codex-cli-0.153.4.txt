Cobra considers a command to have an available subcommand when **at least one direct child passes `IsAvailableCommand()`** (`command.go:1615–1629`, `HasAvailableSubCommands`).

That child must:

- Have an empty `Deprecated` field and not be `Hidden`.
- Not be its parent’s designated `helpCommand`.
- Be `Runnable()` **or recursively have an available subcommand**.

These checks appear in `command.go:1560–1576`, `IsAvailableCommand`. If no child qualifies, `HasAvailableSubCommands()` returns `false`; its comment explicitly ties this check to the default usage/help template’s “available commands” section.
