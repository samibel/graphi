`shell_completions.go` provides flag annotations used by shell completion. `MarkFlagRequired` marks a flag with `BashCompOneRequiredFlag=true`, causing completion to prioritize it and command execution to require it (`shell_completions.go:21-23`, `shell_completions.go:35-40`). `MarkFlagFilename` and `MarkPersistentFlagFilename` restrict completion to specified file extensions for local and persistent flags respectively (`shell_completions.go:42-46`, `shell_completions.go:58-63`).
