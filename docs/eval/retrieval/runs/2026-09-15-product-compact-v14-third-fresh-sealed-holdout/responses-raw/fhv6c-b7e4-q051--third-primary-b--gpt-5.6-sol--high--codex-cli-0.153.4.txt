Use `GenMarkdown` instead of `GenMarkdownTree`; the latter generates one file for every command in the tree (`site/content/docgen/md.md:29-57`).

For customized single-command output, use:

```go
var out bytes.Buffer
err := doc.GenMarkdownCustom(cmd, &out, linkHandler)
```
