Use `GenManTreeFromOpts(cmd, opts)` from `doc/man_docs.go`, setting `opts.Path` to the destination directory and optionally supplying `opts.Header`. Its documented contract is to generate a man page for the command and all descendants, writing pages into `opts.Path` (`doc/man_docs.go:46–49`). A nil header is replaced with `&GenManHeader{}`; child iteration skips unavailable commands and additional help topics (`doc/man_docs.go:48–56`). The returned `error` should be checked. `GenManTree` is also documented as a tree-generation entry point whose header may be nil (`doc/man_docs.go:33–34`).
