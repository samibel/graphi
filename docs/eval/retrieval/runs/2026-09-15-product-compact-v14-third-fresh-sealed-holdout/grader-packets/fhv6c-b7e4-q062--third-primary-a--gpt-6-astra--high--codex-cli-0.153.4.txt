To create a Bash alias with completions, use:

```bash
alias aliasname=origcommand
complete -o default -F __start_origcommand aliasname
```

Replace `origcommand` with your program’s command name and `aliasname` with your chosen alias. The “Aliases” section explicitly documents this setup in `site/content/completions/_index.md:402–408`.
