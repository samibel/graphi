`(*Command).AddCommand(cmds...)` loops over the children, rejects adding the command to itself, assigns each child's `parent` field to the receiver, updates cached maximum use/path/name lengths, propagates any global normalization function, appends the child to `c.commands`, and marks that slice unsorted (`command.go:1302-1330`). `Parent()` simply returns that stored pointer (`command.go:1842-1845`).
