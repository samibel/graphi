If `SetIn` was not called, `getIn(def)` recursively checks the command’s parent (`c.parent.getIn(def)`) while `HasParent()` is true. It therefore inherits the nearest ancestor’s non-nil `inReader`; if no ancestor has one, it returns the provided default reader (`command.go:414–422`). `SetIn` stores the reader in `c.inReader` (`command.go:300–304`), and `HasParent` means `c.parent != nil` (`command.go:1631–1634`).
