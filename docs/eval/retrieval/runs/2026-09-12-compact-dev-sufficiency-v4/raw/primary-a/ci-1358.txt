After defining the flags, call `cmd.MarkFlagsMutuallyExclusive("first", "second")` with their names. This method annotates the set so invocation with more than one of its flags produces an error (`flag_groups.go:62–66`); the rule is at most one, so it does not itself require either flag. Group processing only considers a group when all its flags are defined (`flag_groups.go:128–130`). Completion also hides the other mutually exclusive flags once one is present (`flag_groups.go:223–224`, `273–274`).
