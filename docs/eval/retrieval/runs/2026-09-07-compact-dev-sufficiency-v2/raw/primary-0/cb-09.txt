shell_completions.go includes helpers controlling shell completion behavior. MarkFlagFilename delegates to MarkFlagFilename(c.Flags(), name, extensions...) to restrict a flag's completions to specified extensions (lines 42–46); MarkPersistentFlagFilename does the same with c.PersistentFlags() (lines 58–63). The MarkFlagRequired comment says required flags receive completion priority (lines 35–36).
