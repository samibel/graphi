`enforceFlagGroupsForCompletion` adjusts flag metadata so ordinary completion produces valid choices: when no flag in a one-required group is present, it marks all members required; when one member of a mutually-exclusive group is present, it hides the others (`flag_groups.go:220-226`, `flag_groups.go:273`). Group state comes from annotations: `processFlagForGroupAnnotation` ignores incomplete groups, initializes every member as unset, and records each flag's `Changed` state (`flag_groups.go:121-142`). It does nothing when flag parsing is disabled (`flag_groups.go:225-226`).
