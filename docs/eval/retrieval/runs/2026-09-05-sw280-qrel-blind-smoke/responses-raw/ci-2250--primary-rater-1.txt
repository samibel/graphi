You use a single dash (-) for shorthand flags (single-letter flags), while you use double dashes (--) for long flags (multi-letter flags). The documentation in site/content/user_guide.md (lines 577-589) shows examples: -h is the short form of --help, and -l is the short form of --license. The test in command_test.go:1256 also references shorthand flag handling with the pattern "unknown shorthand flag: 'v' in -v".
