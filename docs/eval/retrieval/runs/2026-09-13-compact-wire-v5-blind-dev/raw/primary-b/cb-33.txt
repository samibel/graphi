`(*Command).Flags()` returns the complete `pflag.FlagSet` applicable to the command: local flags plus persistent flags declared on it and its parents (`command.go:1641-1653`). It lazily creates the set with `ContinueOnError` and directs flag errors to the command's buffer. `InheritedFlags` returns the parent-derived subset after merging persistent flags (`command.go:1695-1701`), while `ResetFlags` replaces local/persistent sets and clears cached derived sets (`command.go:1737-1749`).
