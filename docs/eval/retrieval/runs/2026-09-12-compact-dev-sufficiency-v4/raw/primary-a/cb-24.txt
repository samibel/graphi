`(*Command).enforceFlagGroupsForCompletion()` adjusts flag metadata so normal completion can enforce group rules (`flag_groups.go:220–228`). When a required-together group has a present member, the other members are marked required; when no member of a one-required group is present, all its flags are marked required; when a mutually exclusive member is present, the other members are hidden. Hidden mutually exclusive flags are consequently not suggested (`flag_groups.go:273–274`), and required flags are prioritized in completion (`shell_completions.go:21–22`). The method exits immediately if `DisableFlagParsing` is true (`flag_groups.go:225–228`).
