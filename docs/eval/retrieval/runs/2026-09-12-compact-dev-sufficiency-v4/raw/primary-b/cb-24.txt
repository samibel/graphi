`(*Command).enforceFlagGroupsForCompletion` adjusts flag metadata so ordinary completion can honor groups (`flag_groups.go:220–228`): when a flag in a group is present, the other group flags are marked required; when no flags in a one-required group are present, all group flags are marked required; and when a mutually exclusive flag is present, the other flags in its group are hidden. The latter prevents those conflicting flags from being suggested (`flag_groups.go:273–274`). Required flags are prioritized by shell completion (`shell_completions.go:21–22`). Enforcement returns immediately when `DisableFlagParsing` is true (`flag_groups.go:225–228`).
