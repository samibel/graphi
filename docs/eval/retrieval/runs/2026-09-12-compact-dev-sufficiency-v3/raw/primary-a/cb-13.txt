`SuggestionsFor` scans available child commands and adds a command name when `ld(typedName, cmd.Name(), true)` is at most `SuggestionsMinimumDistance` or the command name has the typed name as a case-insensitive prefix; a case-insensitive match in the child's `SuggestFor` list also adds its name (`command.go:832–850`). `findSuggestions` returns nothing when `DisableSuggestions` is true, defaults a nonpositive distance to 2, and formats matches under `Did you mean this?` (`command.go:750–762`).
