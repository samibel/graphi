A command’s `GroupID` assigns that subcommand to a command group for display in its parent’s help output (`command.go:73-75`). Groups have an `ID` and display `Title` (`command.go:41-45`) and are registered on the parent with `AddGroup` (`command.go:1356-1359`).

The built-in help and completion commands can be assigned similarly through `SetHelpCommandGroupID` and `SetCompletionCommandGroupID` (`command.go:332-345`).
