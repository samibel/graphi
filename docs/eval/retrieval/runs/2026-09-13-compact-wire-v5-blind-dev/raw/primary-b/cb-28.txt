`Command.GroupID` assigns a subcommand to a named group in its parent's help output (`command.go:73-75`). Groups themselves have an `ID` and display `Title`, are added to a parent with `AddGroup`, and can be queried with `Groups`/`ContainsGroup` (`command.go:41-46`, `command.go:1331-1359`). Separate setters place Cobra's default help and completion commands into a requested group (`command.go:332-346`).
