`doc/man_docs.go` supports man-page generation: the comments identify `GenMan` and `GenManTreeOptions` (lines 103 and 82–83). Its `fillHeader` helper (lines 118–141) supplies an uppercase title with spaces replaced by escaped hyphens, defaults the section to `1`, uses the current time or parsed `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` when the date is absent, formats the date as `Jan 2006`, and supplies `Auto generated by spf13/cobra` when Source is empty and auto-generation is enabled. Invalid epoch values return an error.
