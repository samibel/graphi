Use `cmd.Flags()` for the command's applicable flag set; persistent flags from the command and its parents are merged into it by `mergePersistentFlags` (`command.go:1643`, `command.go:1847-1853`). If you need categories separately, use `cmd.LocalFlags()` for local flags (`command.go:1669-1670`) and `cmd.InheritedFlags()` for parent-derived flags (`command.go:1695-1718`). Iterate a returned `pflag.FlagSet` with `VisitAll`, as the inherited-set implementation itself does (`command.go:1711-1717`).
