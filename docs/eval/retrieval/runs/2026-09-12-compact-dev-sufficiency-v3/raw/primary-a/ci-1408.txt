For a global flag declared persistently on a parent, access it through the subcommand's applicable flag set, for example `subCmd.Flags().Lookup("flag-name")` or `subCmd.Flag("flag-name")`. `Flags` is documented to include local and persistent flags declared on the command and all parents (`command.go:1641–1643`); `Flag` begins with that lookup (`1795–1796`). A parent's local nonpersistent flags are explicitly documented as not persisting to subcommands (`1655–1656`).
