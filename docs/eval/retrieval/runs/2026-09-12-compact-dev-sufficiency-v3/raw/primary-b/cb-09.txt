shell_completions.go provides flag helpers for shell completion. MarkFlagRequired delegates to the FlagSet helper and its documentation says the flag is prioritized in completion and required when invoking the command (lines 21-27). MarkPersistentFlagRequired documents the corresponding persistent-flag behavior (lines 29-30). MarkFlagFilename restricts completion to specified extensions, and MarkPersistentFlagFilename applies that helper to PersistentFlags() (lines 42-43 and 58-62).
