- **`ValidArgs`** supplies a list of values; `OnlyValidArgs` checks positional arguments against that list, stripping tab-separated descriptions (`args.go:49–65`). For shell completion, it applies to the **first argument only** (`zsh_completions.go:61–63`).
- **`ValidArgsFunction`** is a callback taking `cmd`, `args`, and `toComplete`, returning `[]string` and `ShellCompDirective` (`command.go:87`). It supports completion for **any argument**, including the first (`zsh_completions.go:61–63`).

**They cannot both be used on one command.** `command.go:86` explicitly states: “Only one of ValidArgs and ValidArgsFunction can be used for a command.”
