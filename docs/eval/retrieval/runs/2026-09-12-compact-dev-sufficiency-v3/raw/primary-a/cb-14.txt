Shell completion scripts request results through the hidden command represented by `ShellCompRequestCmd`, or `ShellCompNoDescRequestCmd` for results without descriptions (`completions.go:27–31`). Its `Run` handler dispatches the supplied arguments to `cmd.getCompletions(args)`, receiving the final command, completions, directive, and error (`completions.go:202–206`). Errors are reported with `CompErrorln`, but processing continues so valid completions and the directive can still be emitted; `CalledAs()` determines whether the no-description command was used (`207–215`).
