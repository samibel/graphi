Use cmd.RegisterFlagCompletionFunc(flagName, callback), where callback has signature func(*Command, []string, string) ([]string, ShellCompDirective). It rejects nonexistent flags and duplicate registrations, then stores the callback under the flag pointer while holding a mutex (completions.go:132-146). GetFlagCompletionFunc retrieves it under a read lock (completions.go:148-161). The comment in shell_completions.go:52-53 says these Go completion functions work across all shells.
