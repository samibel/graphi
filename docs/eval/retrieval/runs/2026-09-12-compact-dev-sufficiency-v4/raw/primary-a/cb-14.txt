Shell scripts request completion through the hidden `__complete` command (`ShellCompRequestCmd`, `completions.go:27–29`). `initCompleteCmd` constructs that command with `ShellCompNoDescRequestCmd` as an alias, `Hidden: true`, `DisableFlagParsing: true`, and `Args: MinimumNArgs(1)` (`completions.go:193–201`). Its `Run` callback forwards the supplied command-line arguments to `cmd.getCompletions(args)`, receiving the final command, completions, directive, and error; a returned error is reported through `CompErrorln(err.Error())` (`completions.go:202–208`).
