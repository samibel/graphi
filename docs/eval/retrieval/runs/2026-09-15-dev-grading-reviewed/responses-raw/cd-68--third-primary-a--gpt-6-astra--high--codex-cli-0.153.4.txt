`CompErrorln(msg string)` prints a completion error to stderr with a trailing newline.

- It calls `CompError(fmt.Sprintf("%s\n", msg))` (`completions.go:898–901`).
- `CompError` adds `[Error] ` and calls `CompDebug(msg, true)` (`completions.go:892–896`).
- `CompDebug` adds `[Debug] `, prints to stderr, and also appends to `BASH_COMP_DEBUG_FILE` if set and successfully opened (`completions.go:860–882`).

Thus, `CompErrorln("oops")` produces:
```text
[Debug] [Error] oops
```
followed by a newline.
