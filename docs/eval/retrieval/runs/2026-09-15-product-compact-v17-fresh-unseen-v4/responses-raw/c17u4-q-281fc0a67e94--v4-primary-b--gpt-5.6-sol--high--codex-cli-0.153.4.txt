`ResetFlags` rebuilds:

- `c.flagErrorBuf` as a new `bytes.Buffer`, then resets it.
- `c.flags` as a new `flag.FlagSet` named with `c.Name()`, using `flag.ContinueOnError`, with output directed to `c.flagErrorBuf`.
- `c.pflags` identically as a new persistent `flag.FlagSet`, sharing that error buffer.

It invalidates the cached flag sets by setting these to `nil`:

- `c.lflags` — local flags
- `c.iflags` — inherited flags
- `c.parentsPflags` — parent persistent flags

Evidence: `command.go:1736–1749`, specifically the assignments in `(*Command).ResetFlags`. The snippets at `command.go:1667–1718` show that `LocalFlags` and `InheritedFlags` lazily rebuild `lflags` and `iflags`; `command.go:1846–1853` shows `parentsPflags` is refreshed during persistent-flag merging.
