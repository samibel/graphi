Call doc.GenManTree(cmd, nil, dir) and handle its returned error; nil is allowed for the header. It generates pages for the command and its descendants in the specified directory (doc/man_docs.go:33-44). For options, use GenManTreeFromOpts with GenManTreeOptions; GenManTree itself passes Header, Path, and CommandSeparator "-". Hyphens in command names can create ambiguous filenames, as documented at lines 35-37.
