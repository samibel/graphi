Use the `doc` package’s `GenManTree` on the root command. It generates a man page for that command and every descendant in the specified directory; the header may be `nil` (`doc/man_docs.go:33-34`, symbol `GenManTree`).

Alternatively, `GenManTreeFromOpts` does the same and writes pages to `opts.Path` (`doc/man_docs.go:46-47`).
