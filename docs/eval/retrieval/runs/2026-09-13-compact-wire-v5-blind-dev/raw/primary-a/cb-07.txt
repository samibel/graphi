`flag_groups.go` implements Cobra's grouped-flag annotations and validation. It defines annotation keys for required-together, one-required, and mutually-exclusive groups (`flag_groups.go:25-29`), has helpers such as `hasAllFlags` (`flag_groups.go:111-119`), and validates required-together groups by detecting a partially set group, sorting the missing names, and returning a deterministic error (`flag_groups.go:144-165`). `ValidateFlagGroups` is the command entry point and skips work when flag parsing is disabled (`flag_groups.go:81-82`).
