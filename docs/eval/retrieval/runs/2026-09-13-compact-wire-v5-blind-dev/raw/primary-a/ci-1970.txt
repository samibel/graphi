Use `cmd.Flags()` for the command's effective flag set; persistent flags from the command and its parents are merged into it by `mergePersistentFlags` (`command.go:1643`, `command.go:1847-1853`). For separate views, `cmd.LocalFlags()` returns locally defined flags (`command.go:1669-1670`), while `cmd.InheritedFlags()` builds and returns parent flags that are not shadowed locally (`command.go:1695-1718`). Iterate the returned `pflag.FlagSet` to obtain the list.
