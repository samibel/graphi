The documented unsupported mechanisms are:

- Legacy custom completions written in Bash, including the `BashCompCustom` flag annotation. Zsh ignores them. Replace them with `ValidArgsFunction` and `RegisterFlagCompletionFunc()`.
- `MarkFlagCustom()`. Zsh ignores it. Replace it with `RegisterFlagCompletionFunc()`.
