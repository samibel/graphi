After defining the flags on the command, call `cmd.MarkFlagsMutuallyExclusive("flag-a", "flag-b", ...)`. Cobra annotates every named flag as one mutually-exclusive group and errors if more than one is supplied (`flag_groups.go:62-78`). Every named flag must already exist; otherwise this method panics (`flag_groups.go:67-71`). Separate calls create separate groups and allow a flag to belong to multiple groups (`flag_groups.go:72-76`).
