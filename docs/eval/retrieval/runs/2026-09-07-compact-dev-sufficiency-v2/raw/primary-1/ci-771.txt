Use `ValidArgsFunction` and `RegisterFlagCompletionFunc()` for custom completions that work across bash, zsh, fish, and PowerShell (`site/content/completions/_index.md:477–479`). Those lines explicitly recommend these portable APIs and state that legacy Bash-script completions, `BashCompCustom`, and `MarkFlagCustom()` are ignored for zsh. The supported generated-shell list is also given in `site/content/user_guide.md:742`.
