(*Command).Flags() returns the command's *flag.FlagSet, documented as covering local and persistent flags declared on the command and its parents. It lazily initializes c.flags with ContinueOnError and sends flag output to c.flagErrorBuf (command.go:1641-1653). LocalFlags is for flags declared on the current command, LocalNonPersistentFlags excludes persistent flags, and InheritedFlags first merges persistent flags (command.go:1655-1670 and 1696-1712).
