`AddTemplateFuncs` iterates over the supplied `template.FuncMap` and inserts each key/value pair into the package-level `templateFuncs` registry:

```go
for k, v := range tmplFuncs {
    templateFuncs[k] = v
}
```

Evidence: `cobra.go:89–94`. The registry itself is declared as `var templateFuncs = template.FuncMap{...}` at `cobra.go:31–40`, and is passed to templates via `t.Funcs(templateFuncs)` at `cobra.go:181`.
