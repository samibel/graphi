`SetIn(nil)` makes `InOrStdin()` return the parent command’s input if one exists; otherwise it returns `os.Stdin`.

Evidence: `SetIn` assigns `nil` to `c.inReader` (`command.go:300–304`), `InOrStdin` calls `c.getIn(os.Stdin)` (`command.go:389–392`), and `getIn` falls back through the parent before returning its default (`command.go:414–422`).
