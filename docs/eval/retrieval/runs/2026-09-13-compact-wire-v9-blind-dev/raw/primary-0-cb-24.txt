During completion, `completions.go:379-381` calls `finalCmd.enforceFlagGroupsForCompletion()` before generating flag completions.

That method:

- Returns immediately if `c.DisableFlagParsing` is true (`flag_groups.go:226-228`).
- Marks the other flags in a group required when one member is present.
- Marks every flag in a one-required group required when none is present.
- Hides the other members of a mutually exclusive group when one is set, preventing shell completion from suggesting them (`flag_groups.go:220-225`, `flag_groups.go:273-277`).

Thus, flag groups are enforced by translating their constraints into the standard completion metadata—required or hidden—before completion candidates are calculated.
