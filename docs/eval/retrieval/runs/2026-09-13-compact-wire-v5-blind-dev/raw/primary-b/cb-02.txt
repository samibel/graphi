`(*Command).MarkFlagsMutuallyExclusive(flagNames...)` merges persistent flags, verifies every named flag exists, and annotates each with the space-joined group under `cobra_annotation_mutually_exclusive`. A missing flag or annotation error causes a panic; repeated calls create separate group entries, so a flag can belong to multiple groups (`flag_groups.go:63-78`; annotation constant at `flag_groups.go:25-29`).
