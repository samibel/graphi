The four boolean switches in `CompletionOptions` (`completions.go:105–116`) are:

- **`DisableDefaultCmd`**: Prevents creation of the default `completion` command (lines 106–107).
- **`DisableNoDescFlag`**: Prevents creation of `--no-descriptions` for shells supporting descriptions (lines 108–110).
- **`DisableDescriptions`**: Turns off all completion descriptions for shells supporting them (lines 111–113).
- **`HiddenDefaultCmd`**: Hides the default `completion` command (lines 114–115).
