Cobra identifies required flags through an annotation, then offers those not already set:

- `MarkFlagRequired` sets `BashCompOneRequiredFlag` to `[]string{"true"}` (`shell_completions.go:38–40`). `MarkPersistentFlagRequired` applies this to persistent flags (`shell_completions.go:31–33`).
- `completeRequireFlags` checks whether that annotation key exists and whether `flag.Changed` is false. Matching flags are passed to `getFlagNameCompletions(flag, toComplete)` to produce suggestions (`completions.go:570–576`). It checks annotation **presence**, not its value.
- It visits both `finalCmd.InheritedFlags()` and `finalCmd.NonInheritedFlags()` (`completions.go:582–587`). The preceding comment explains that `Flags()` may not have merged these sets when flag parsing is disabled (`completions.go:579–581`).
