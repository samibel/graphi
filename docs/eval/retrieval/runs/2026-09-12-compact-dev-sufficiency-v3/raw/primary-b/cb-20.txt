Persistent flags propagate through mergePersistentFlags: it calls updateParentsPflags, then adds both the command's PersistentFlags() and the collected parentsPflags to c.Flags() (command.go:1849-1853). The updateParentsPflags comment explicitly says it adds new persistent flags from all parents and creates the collection if needed (command.go:1855-1863). LocalFlags triggers this merge; LocalNonPersistentFlags filters out persistent flags and is explicitly documented as not propagating to subcommands (command.go:1655-1670).
