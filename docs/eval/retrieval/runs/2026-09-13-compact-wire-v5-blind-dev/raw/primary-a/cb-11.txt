Required flags are checked by `(*Command).ValidateRequiredFlags` in `command.go`. Unless flag parsing is disabled, it scans `c.Flags()` for `BashCompOneRequiredFlag` annotations whose flag was not changed, then returns `required flag(s) ... not set` listing all missing names (`command.go:1146-1169`). Flags become required through `MarkFlagRequired`, which applies that annotation (`shell_completions.go:21-26`).
