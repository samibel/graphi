Completion calls `enforceFlagGroupsForCompletion`, which is a no-op when flag parsing is disabled (`flag_groups.go:220-226`). It derives per-group status from annotations: `processFlagForGroupAnnotation` splits each encoded group, ignores groups unless all flags exist, initializes every member as unset, and records each member's `Changed` state (`flag_groups.go:121-142`). The resulting enforcement marks every member of an unsatisfied one-required group as required and hides the remaining members after a mutually-exclusive flag is present, allowing normal completion filtering to enforce the groups (`flag_groups.go:220-226`, `flag_groups.go:273`).
