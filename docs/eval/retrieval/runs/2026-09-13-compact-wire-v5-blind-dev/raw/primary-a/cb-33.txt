`(*Command).Flags()` returns the complete flag set applicable to the command—local flags plus persistent flags declared on it and its parents. It lazily creates that `FlagSet`, creates a shared error buffer if needed, and directs flag errors there (`command.go:1641-1653`). Related views include `LocalNonPersistentFlags` for flags that do not propagate and `InheritedFlags` for parent-provided flags (`command.go:1655-1659`, `command.go:1695-1701`). `ResetFlags` recreates the local and persistent sets and clears cached derived sets (`command.go:1737-1749`).
