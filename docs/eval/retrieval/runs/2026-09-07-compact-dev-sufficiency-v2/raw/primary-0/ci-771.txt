Use ValidArgsFunction and RegisterFlagCompletionFunc() for custom completions portable across bash, zsh, fish, and PowerShell; site/content/completions/_index.md:477–479 explicitly recommends these APIs. Those lines also state that legacy Bash-script custom completions, BashCompCustom annotations, and MarkFlagCustom() are ignored for zsh. The supported generated shell-completion files are listed in site/content/user_guide.md:742.
