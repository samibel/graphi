`hasSeeAlso` returns `true` when either:

- The command has a parent (`cmd.HasParent()`), or
- It has at least one child from `cmd.Commands()` that is available and is not an additional help-topic command.

Otherwise, it returns `false` ([`doc/util.go`, lines 22–37]). `IsAvailableCommand` excludes deprecated, hidden, and autogenerated help commands, and requires the child to be runnable or have available subcommands ([`command.go`, lines 1559–1576]).
