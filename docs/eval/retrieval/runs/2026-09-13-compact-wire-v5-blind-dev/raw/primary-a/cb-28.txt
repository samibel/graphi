A command's group ID associates it with one of its parent's registered command groups, whose definitions are `Group{ID, Title}` and are installed with `AddGroup` (`command.go:41-46`, `command.go:1356-1359`). `ContainsGroup` checks whether a given ID is registered (`command.go:1346-1354`). Cobra also lets you assign the generated help and completion commands to groups via `SetHelpCommandGroupID` and `SetCompletionCommandGroupID`; the configured IDs are retained for defaults not yet created (`command.go:332-346`).
