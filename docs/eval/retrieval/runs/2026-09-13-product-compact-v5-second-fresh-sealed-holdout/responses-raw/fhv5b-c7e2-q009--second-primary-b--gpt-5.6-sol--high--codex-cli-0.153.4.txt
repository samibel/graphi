`AddTemplateFuncs` iterates over the supplied `template.FuncMap` and assigns every key/value pair into the package-level `templateFuncs` map:

```go
for k, v := range tmplFuncs {
    templateFuncs[k] = v
}
```

Thus, new names are added and existing names are overwritten by the supplied functions (`cobra.go:88–95`). The destination map is the built-in `templateFuncs` map declared at `cobra.go:32–40`.
