Persistent flags declared on a command are held in its `PersistentFlags()` set (`command.go:1725–1727`). Propagation is handled by `mergePersistentFlags`, whose comment says it adds missing persistent flags of all parents; it calls `updateParentsPflags()` and adds the current command's persistent set to `c.Flags()` (`command.go:1848–1851`). `LocalFlags()` invokes that merge (`command.go:1669–1670`). The separate `persistentFlag(name)` helper recursively finds a matching persistent flag (`command.go:1805–1807`); the excerpts do not show the parent traversal or collision implementation.
