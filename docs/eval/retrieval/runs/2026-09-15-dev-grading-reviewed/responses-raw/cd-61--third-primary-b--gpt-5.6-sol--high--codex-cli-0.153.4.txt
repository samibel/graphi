`ShellCompDirective` is an `int` bit map controlling shell-completion behavior (`completions.go:41–43`). Its recognized flags are:

- `ShellCompDirectiveError`
- `ShellCompDirectiveNoSpace`
- `ShellCompDirectiveNoFileComp`
- `ShellCompDirectiveFilterFileExt`
- `ShellCompDirectiveFilterDirs`
- `ShellCompDirectiveKeepOrder`

The `string()` method lists active flags, returns `ShellCompDirectiveDefault` when none are set, and reports values at or above `shellCompDirectiveMaxValue` as errors (`completions.go:162–191`). The comments specifically say `Error` discards completions and `NoSpace` prevents adding a trailing space (`completions.go:54–63`).
