Yes, there is a way to customize the entire deprecation message for a command.

The deprecation message is customized by setting the `Deprecated` field on the `cobra.Command` struct to your custom message string. Evidence from the response shows:

Both examples show that the entire deprecation message can be fully customized through the `Deprecated` field when defining a Command.
