A command’s `GroupID` assigns that subcommand to a command group in its parent’s help output (`command.go:73-75`). Groups have an `ID` and display `Title` (`command.go:41-45`) and are registered on the parent with `AddGroup` (`command.go:1356-1359`).

The help and completion commands can be assigned similarly through `SetHelpCommandGroupID` (`command.go:332-339`) and `SetCompletionCommandGroupID` (`command.go:341-345`).
