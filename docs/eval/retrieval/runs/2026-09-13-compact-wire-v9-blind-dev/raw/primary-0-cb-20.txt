Persistent flags propagate when a child’s applicable flags are assembled:

- `PersistentFlags()` stores flags declared persistently on the current command (`command.go:1725-1735`).
- `mergePersistentFlags()` calls `updateParentsPflags()`, then adds both the command’s own persistent flags and `parentsPflags` to `c.Flags()` (`command.go:1847-1853`).
- Individual lookup similarly checks the current command first, then refreshes and searches `parentsPflags` (`persistentFlag`, `command.go:1805-1816`).

Thus, a child receives missing persistent flags collected from its parents, while non-persistent local flags explicitly do not propagate (`LocalNonPersistentFlags`, `command.go:1654-1666`).
