`shell_completions.go` provides flag annotations used by shell completion. `MarkFlagRequired` marks a flag with `BashCompOneRequiredFlag=true`, which both prioritizes it in completion and makes omission an invocation error (`shell_completions.go:21-23`, `shell_completions.go:35-40`). It also exposes methods that annotate local or persistent flags with permitted filename extensions (`shell_completions.go:42-46`, `shell_completions.go:58-63`).
