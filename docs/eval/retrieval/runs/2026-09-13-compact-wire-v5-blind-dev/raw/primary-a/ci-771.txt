Implement custom completion in Go with the command's `ValidArgsFunction` for arguments and `RegisterFlagCompletionFunc` for flags. Those APIs are portable across bash, zsh, fish, and PowerShell, unlike legacy Bash-script custom completions, which zsh ignores (`site/content/completions/_index.md:477-478`). A shell script can request results from the executable through the hidden `__complete` command, or `__completeNoDesc` when descriptions are unwanted (`completions.go:25-34`). Cobra can generate scripts for all four listed shells (`site/content/user_guide.md:742`).
