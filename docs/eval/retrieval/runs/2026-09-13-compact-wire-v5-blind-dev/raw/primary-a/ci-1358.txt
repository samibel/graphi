After defining the flags, call `cmd.MarkFlagsMutuallyExclusive("flagA", "flagB", ...)`. Cobra merges persistent flags, verifies every name exists, and annotates every member with the space-joined group; invoking the command with more than one member then errors (`flag_groups.go:62-78`). A missing flag name causes a panic, and separate calls create separate groups so a flag can belong to multiple groups (`flag_groups.go:67-77`).
