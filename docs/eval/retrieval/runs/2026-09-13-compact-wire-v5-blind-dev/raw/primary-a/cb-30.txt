Call `GenManTree(root, header, dir)` for the simple API (`doc/man_docs.go:33-38`), or `GenManTreeFromOpts(root, opts)` when you need an output path or custom command separator. The options version recursively generates all available, non-help-topic descendants, defaults the section to `1` and separator to `_`, writes each page as `<command_path>.<section>` under `opts.Path`, and delegates page rendering to `GenMan` (`doc/man_docs.go:46-80`). The header may be nil (`doc/man_docs.go:33-34`).
