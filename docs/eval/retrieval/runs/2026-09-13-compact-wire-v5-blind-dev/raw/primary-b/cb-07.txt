`flag_groups.go` implements Cobra's flag-group annotations and validation. It defines annotation keys for required-together, one-required, and mutually-exclusive groups (`flag_groups.go:25-29`), includes helpers such as `hasAllFlags` (`flag_groups.go:111-119`), and validates required-together groups by reporting partially set groups and their sorted missing flags (`flag_groups.go:144-165`). `ValidateFlagGroups` is the command-level entry point and bypasses validation when flag parsing is disabled (`flag_groups.go:81-82`).
