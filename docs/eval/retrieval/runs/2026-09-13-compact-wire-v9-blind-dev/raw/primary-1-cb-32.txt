`(*Command).Execute()` calls `c.ExecuteC()`, discards the returned command, and returns only its error (`command.go:1035–1041`). `ExecuteC()` initializes a missing context with `context.Background()` and redirects execution to the root command when called on a child (`command.go:1051–1062`).
