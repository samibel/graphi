Check that the command's `Version` field is nonempty, for example `cmd.Version = "1.0.0"`. `InitDefaultVersionFlag` returns immediately when `c.Version == ""` (`command.go:1202–1207`). With a nonempty version and no existing `version` flag, Cobra adds a boolean flag that prints `Version`, plus `-v` if that shorthand is free (`command.go:108–112`). An existing version flag is left unchanged (`1202`). The response does not show your command configuration, so it cannot establish which condition applies to your case.
