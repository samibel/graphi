Call `doc.GenManTree(cmd, header, dir)` for the simple API; it generates a page for the command and every descendant in the given directory, and `header` may be nil (`doc/man_docs.go:33-38`). For control over output path, header, and command separator, call `GenManTreeFromOpts`; it recursively skips unavailable/help-topic children, defaults the section to `1` and separator to `_`, creates each `<command-path>.<section>` file, and calls `GenMan` (`doc/man_docs.go:46-80`).
