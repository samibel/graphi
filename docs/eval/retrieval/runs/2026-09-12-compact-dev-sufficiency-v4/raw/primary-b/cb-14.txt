Shell completion scripts request results through the hidden command named `__complete`, defined by `ShellCompRequestCmd` (`completions.go:27–29`). `initCompleteCmd` constructs this special command with a `ShellCompNoDescRequestCmd` alias, `Hidden: true`, `DisableFlagParsing: true`, and `Args: MinimumNArgs(1)` (`completions.go:193–201`). Its `Run` callback dispatches the supplied arguments to `cmd.getCompletions(args)`, receiving the final command, completions, directive, and error; errors are passed to `CompErrorln` (`completions.go:202–208`).
