`Command.ResetFlags` clears and recreates:

- The flag error buffer: `c.flagErrorBuf` becomes a new `bytes.Buffer` and is reset.
- The local flag set: `c.flags` becomes a new `flag.FlagSet`.
- The persistent flag set: `c.pflags` becomes a new `flag.FlagSet`.
- Both new flag sets send output to `c.flagErrorBuf`.
- Cached/inherited flag state is cleared by setting `c.lflags`, `c.iflags`, and `c.parentsPflags` to `nil`.

Evidence: `command.go:1737–1749`, in `func (c *Command) ResetFlags()`.
