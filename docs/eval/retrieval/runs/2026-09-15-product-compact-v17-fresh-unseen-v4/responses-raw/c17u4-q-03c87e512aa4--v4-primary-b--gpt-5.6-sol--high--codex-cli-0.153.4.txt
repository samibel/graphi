`hasSeeAlso` returns `true` when either:

- The command has a parent (`cmd.HasParent()`), or
- It has at least one child from `cmd.Commands()` that is available and is not an additional help-topic command.

It skips children where `!c.IsAvailableCommand()` or `c.IsAdditionalHelpTopicCommand()`; otherwise, the first qualifying child makes it return `true`. If neither condition holds, it returns `false` ([`doc/util.go`, lines 22–37]).

“Available” excludes deprecated, hidden, and autogenerated help commands, and requires the command to be runnable or have available subcommands ([`command.go`, lines 1559–1576]).
