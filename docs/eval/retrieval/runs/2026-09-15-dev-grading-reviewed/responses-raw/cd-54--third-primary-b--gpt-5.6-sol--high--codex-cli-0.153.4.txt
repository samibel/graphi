Call `SetUsageTemplate` on your command with your template string:

```go
cmd.SetUsageTemplate(myTemplate)
```

This is documented in `site/content/user_guide.md:583-591`. The implementation stores the string in `Command.usageTemplate` (`command.go:311-314`), and the default `UsageFunc` renders `c.UsageTemplate()` (`command.go:424-441`).
