Use `ValidArgsFunction` for custom argument completion and `RegisterFlagCompletionFunc()` for custom flag completion: the documentation explicitly recommends them as portable across Bash, Zsh, Fish, and PowerShell (`site/content/completions/_index.md:477–478`). Those are also the listed shell-generation targets (`site/content/user_guide.md:742–743`). For an additional, unlisted shell, the response identifies the `__complete` request command (`completions.go:28–29`) but does not provide enough protocol or shell-integration detail to implement its adapter.
