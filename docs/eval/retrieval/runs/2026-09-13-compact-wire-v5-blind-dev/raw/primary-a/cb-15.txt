During command execution, Cobra initializes the help and version flags at the last possible point so applications can override them (`command.go:883-886`). `InitDefaultVersionFlag` does nothing if `Command.Version` is empty or a `version` flag already exists; otherwise it creates `--version`, adds `-v` only if that shorthand is free, and marks the flag as Cobra-created (`command.go:1200-1225`). Completion detects a changed, Cobra-created version flag through its annotation (`completions.go:522-533`).
