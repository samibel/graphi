`--version` only exists when the root command’s `Version` field is non-empty. The user guide states that Cobra adds the top-level flag “if the Version field is set” (`site/content/user_guide.md:590-592`), and `InitDefaultVersionFlag` explicitly does nothing when `c.Version` is empty (`command.go:1202-1204`). Therefore, `--version` does not work because the root command’s `Version` field was not set.
