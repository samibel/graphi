They were replaced by `ValidArgsFunction`:

- For positional word choices, use `ValidArgsFunction`.
- For file-extension filtering, use it with `ShellCompDirectiveFilterFileExt`.
- File completion is now the default; disable it per argument with `ShellCompDirectiveNoFileComp`.

The old `MarkZshCompPositionalArgumentFile()` and `MarkZshCompPositionalArgumentWords()` helpers are deprecated and silently ignored (`site/content/completions/zsh.md`, lines 25–29).
