During command execution, Cobra initializes help and version flags immediately before parsing so users can override them (`command.go:883-886`). `InitDefaultVersionFlag` does nothing when `Command.Version` is empty or a `version` flag already exists; otherwise it creates `--version`, adds `-v` only when that shorthand is free, and marks the flag as Cobra-created (`command.go:1200-1225`). Completion detects a changed, Cobra-annotated version flag via `helpOrVersionFlagPresent` (`completions.go:522-533`).
