Flags modify command behavior and support POSIX-compliant syntax plus Go’s `flag` package. Commands may define persistent flags inherited by children or flags available only locally (`README.md:68-74`).

In `command.go`:

- `Command.Flags()` returns the complete applicable `FlagSet`, including local and persistent flags from the command and its parents (`command.go:1641-1653`).
- `Command.LocalFlags()` gathers flags set on the current command, excluding inherited parent persistent flags unless shadowed (`command.go:1668-1693`).
- `Command.LocalNonPersistentFlags()` filters local flags to those that do not persist to subcommands (`command.go:1655-1666`).
