Shell completion scripts invoke a hidden command:

- `completions.go:26-33` defines `ShellCompRequestCmd` as `__complete` and `ShellCompNoDescRequestCmd` as `__completeNoDesc`.
- The Bash, Zsh, Fish, and PowerShell generators select `ShellCompRequestCmd` (`bash_completionsV2.go:32`, `zsh_completions.go:88`, `fish_completions.go:31`, `powershell_completions.go:34-35`).
- The hidden command’s `Run` handler dispatches the supplied arguments into the completion engine with `cmd.getCompletions(args)` (`completions.go:205-215`), receiving the final command, completion candidates, directive, and error.
- It detects the no-description variant through `cmd.CalledAs() == ShellCompNoDescRequestCmd` (`completions.go:214`).
