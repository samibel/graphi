## Exact-path sufficiency

The rubric has no special sufficiency exception for an exact-path query. A sufficient answer must satisfy the ordinary PASS conditions, including:

> The response directly and substantively answers the question asked.
>
> Its material factual claims are supported by the exact preserved bundle.
>
> It identifies the essential behavior represented by the reviewed grade-3 spans to the extent needed to answer the question.

It also says:

> Paraphrase, different organization and additional bundle-supported detail are allowed. A response need not copy the reviewed spans verbatim.

Therefore, a response containing the judged span in adjacent pieces counts as sufficient when the pieces together preserve the essential behavior needed to answer the path query. Omitting only an intervening blank line, such as returning lines 24–31 and 33 for a judged span of 24–33, does not make the answer insufficient.
