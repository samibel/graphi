Cobra considers a command to have an available subcommand when at least one entry in `c.commands` returns `true` from `IsAvailableCommand()` (`command.go:1615–1629`).

A subcommand is available when it:

- is not deprecated (`Deprecated` is empty),
- is not hidden,
- is not its parent’s help command, and
- is either runnable or itself has an available subcommand.

This logic is in `Command.IsAvailableCommand()` (`command.go:1560–1576`).
