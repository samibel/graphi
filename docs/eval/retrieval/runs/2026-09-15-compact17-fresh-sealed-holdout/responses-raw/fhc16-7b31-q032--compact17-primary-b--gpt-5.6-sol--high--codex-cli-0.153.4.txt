All direct child commands in `c.commands` that are either:

- available according to `sub.IsAvailableCommand()`, or
- exactly `c.helpCommand`

must have a non-empty `GroupID`.

Evidence: `command.go:1336–1343` checks `(sub.IsAvailableCommand() || sub == c.helpCommand) && sub.GroupID == ""` and returns `false`. `command.go:1559–1576` defines an available command as non-deprecated, non-hidden, not the parent’s help command, and either runnable or having available subcommands.
