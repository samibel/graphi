Persistent flags propagate by being merged into each child command’s complete flag set. In `command.go:1847-1853`, `mergePersistentFlags()`:

1. Calls `updateParentsPflags()` to collect missing persistent flags from all parents.
2. Adds the child’s own `PersistentFlags()` to `c.Flags()`.
3. Adds `c.parentsPflags` to `c.Flags()`.

Individual lookup follows the same hierarchy: `persistentFlag()` first checks the current command’s persistent flags, then updates and searches `parentsPflags` (`command.go:1805-1816`). Non-persistent local flags explicitly do not propagate (`command.go:1654-1666`).
