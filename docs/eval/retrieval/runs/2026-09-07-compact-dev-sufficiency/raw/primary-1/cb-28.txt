`GroupID` selects the group under which a subcommand appears in its parent's help output (`command.go:73`; example assignment in `command_test.go:1866`). The parent maintains groups through `AddGroup(groups ...*Group)` and exposes them through `Groups()` (`command.go:1357–1358,1332–1333`). Dedicated setters exist for the help and completion command group IDs (`command.go:332–334,342–343`).
