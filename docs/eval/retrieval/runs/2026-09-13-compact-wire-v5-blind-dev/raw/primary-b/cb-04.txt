`(*Command).RegisterFlagCompletionFunc` registers a portable Go completion callback with signature `func(cmd *Command, args []string, toComplete string) ([]string, ShellCompDirective)`. It errors if the flag does not exist or already has a callback, otherwise stores the callback in a mutex-protected map keyed by the `*pflag.Flag` (`completions.go:132-148`, `completions.go:35-36`). The registered callback can be retrieved with `GetFlagCompletionFunc` (`completions.go:148-160`).
