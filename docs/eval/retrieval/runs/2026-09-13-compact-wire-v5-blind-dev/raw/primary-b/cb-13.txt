For each available child command, `SuggestionsFor` suggests it when the case-insensitive Levenshtein distance from the typed name is at most `SuggestionsMinimumDistance`, when the command name has the typed name as a case-insensitive prefix, or when the typed name case-insensitively matches an entry in that command's `SuggestFor` list (`command.go:831-851`). `findSuggestions` disables all output when `DisableSuggestions` is set, defaults a nonpositive distance threshold to 2, and formats matches under “Did you mean this?” (`command.go:750-766`).
